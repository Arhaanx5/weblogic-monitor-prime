Alert Fatigue Reduction

Hysteresis & Silent VPN Muting

🚨 70-Socket Hysteresis Thresholding

Eliminates rapid siren toggling (oscillation) when socket counts hover around 70.

  • 1
    Trigger Condition: Sockets breach >70 ➔ Critical alert & siren trigger.
  • 2
    Recovery Zone (65 - 70): Sockets drop to 68 ➔ Remains Critical to avoid rapid toggling.
  • 3
    Clear Condition: Sockets drop below <65 ➔ Alert clears automatically.

📡 Silent VPN Disconnect Protection

  • ✓
